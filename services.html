Services

Four kinds of work, all pointed at the same goal: fewer manual steps, fewer avoidable risks, more time spent on work that actually needs a person.

Service list

Automation IT automation

We find the repetitive, error-prone parts of day-to-day IT work - provisioning, onboarding/offboarding, backups, reporting, routine maintenance - and replace the manual version with scripts, scheduled jobs, or integrations your team can actually read and maintain.

Deliverables are plain: working automation, documentation of what it does and why, and a handover that doesn't leave you dependent on us to keep it running.

Support Managed services & IT support

Day-to-day IT support for small and mid-sized teams - endpoints, accounts, infrastructure basics, and the troubleshooting that otherwise falls on whoever's closest to the problem.

We aim to reduce how often you need us, not increase it: the goal is a stable environment, not a retainer that never shrinks.

Security Cybersecurity

Practical security work: access control, account hygiene, backup and recovery planning, patching discipline, and closing the gaps that cause most real incidents - not theoretical ones.

See the Security & Privacy page for the principles behind how we approach this on every engagement.

Build Custom technology projects

When off-the-shelf tooling doesn't fit, we build the smaller, purpose-built alternative - internal tools, integrations between systems that don't talk to each other, or a focused app for one real workflow.

Our own open-source apps (see Projects) are built the same way we build for clients: offline-first where it makes sense, and privacy considered from the first design decision.

Not sure which of these fits?

That's normal - most engagements start as a conversation about a workflow, not a request for a specific service.
