About Automated Bytes

A background in platform engineering and a reason for building software that keeps people, not just systems, in mind.

Where this started

Health-Flare and Inner Flare exist because of a roughly two-year path to a diagnosis. My son went through that process at SickKids in Toronto before being diagnosed with juvenile idiopathic arthritis. Watching how hard it was to track symptoms consistently - and how much of that information was scattered across notebooks, memory, and apps that didn't respect the family's data - is what led to building Health-Flare, and later Inner Flare, as the kind of tools that should have existed from the start.

Background

Automated Bytes is founder-led, with a working background in platform engineering, site reliability, and automation - the discipline of keeping complex systems running reliably, and of finding the manual steps worth removing before they cause an incident. That same approach carries over into client IT and automation work: pragmatic, built to be maintained, and built with the assumption that the people depending on a system matter more than the system itself.
